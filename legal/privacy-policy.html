1. Who We Are

MILA Hospitality Consultant ("MILA", "we", "our", or "us") provides luxury hotel, resort, restaurant, and hospitality consulting services as well as hospitality talent recruitment through the Mila Hospitality Android app and web app. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use and protect it, and how you can access or delete your data.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information you actively provide or that is required for core app functionality:

  • Account & Authentication Information: Your email address (used to send a 6-digit one-time verification code for passwordless sign-in), display name, and account type (Client / Hotelier or Hospitality Candidate).
  • Consultancy Enquiries: Full name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number, city, property/company name, project scale, selected hospitality service, and project brief notes when you request a consultation.
  • Hospitality Candidate Profiles & Job Applications: Preferred hospitality department/role, years of experience, current property/employer, location, phone number, cover note, saved job bookmarks, and optional CV / resume files (PDF, DOC, or DOCX) that you choose to upload.
  • Client Reviews & Testimonials: Reviewer name, role/property name, rating, and review text submitted for publication after admin review.
  • Optional Notification Token: If you explicitly grant notification permission on Android, we store a Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device token solely to deliver job match alerts, application updates, and enquiry responses.

We do not collect precise GPS location, contacts, call logs, SMS messages, camera/microphone recordings, or background advertising identifiers.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Authenticate your account securely via one-time email verification codes (OTP).
  • Respond to your hotel/restaurant consulting enquiries and coordinate consultations.
  • Match hospitality candidates with relevant hotel, resort, and restaurant job openings and process job applications.
  • Send transactional alerts (such as job alerts or enquiry replies) when you opt in to notifications.
  • Prevent abuse, enforce access controls, and maintain platform reliability.

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information or uploaded resumes to third-party data brokers or advertisers.

4. How Your Data Is Stored & Protected

  • Encryption in Transit: All network traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS / TLS.
  • Row-Level Access Controls (RLS): Database records are protected by strict access policies so users can only view and manage their own profiles, saved jobs, enquiries, and applications, while authorized MILA administrators review incoming enquiries and recruitment applications.
  • Private Resume Storage: Uploaded CV/resume files are stored in a private, access-controlled cloud storage bucket and accessed via short-lived signed links rather than public URLs.

5. Service Providers & Sub-Processors

To run our service reliably, we rely on trusted infrastructure providers under strict security standards:

  • Supabase: Secure cloud database, authentication, and private file storage.
  • Transactional Email Delivery (SMTP / Brevo): Used exclusively to deliver 6-digit login verification codes and account-related emails.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (Android): Used only to deliver optional push notifications if you allow notifications on your device.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your account data, candidate profile, enquiries, and uploaded CV only for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide consultancy and recruitment services.

You may request permanent deletion of your account and associated personal data (including uploaded resumes, candidate profile, saved jobs, and notification tokens) at any time. Verified deletion requests are processed within 48 hours (and no later than 7 business days).

If you believe a child has submitted personal data, please contact us immediately for prompt removal.
